PLXTOGETHER + QUESTIE

Extract the entire ZIP, close WoW, then double-click PlxTogether-Setup.exe.
Approve the Windows prompt, select your Forever client folder, and click Install addons.
Enable PlxTogether, Questie and QuestieDB on character selection.

/plx opens your route. /plx demo tries a simulated party.
Previous and Skip / Next change your target without completing quests.
Popout GPS detaches the arrow. Drag it above your character; Dock GPS in the tracker docks it again.

Manual installation (including macOS): copy the folders inside AddOns into
your game's Interface/AddOns folder. Leave your WTF folder unchanged.

Support contains files used by setup. You do not need to open them.
